# Seattle Dog Park Data Audit — Pass 3

Date: 2026-04-18

## Executive Summary
The core raw-data math mostly reproduces. `scripts/compute_walkshed.py`, `scripts/population_coverage.py`, and `scripts/build_enforcement_datasets.py` all reran cleanly against committed inputs, and the headline base figures behind 11.6%, 76.6%, 30.7 acres, $240,652, 26%, and ~248,900 all checked out. The live PDF is byte-identical to the committed PDF.

The next-class problems are drift and reproducibility gaps around the derived spatial layer, not the raw source tables. The biggest issues are on Part II and in the PDF: the walkshed chart still renders old `9.5 / 78.3` values, the access-gap section mixes incompatible denominators and impossible counts, and the print/PDF enforcement year table still shows obsolete annual totals. Separately, two derived-data pipelines are no longer reproducible from the repo as committed: `citation-rate-by-walkshed-status.csv` and `street-address-geocodes.csv`. A third reproducibility gap remains for the TPL ParkServe cross-tabs, which still have no committed build script. I also found a fresh internal-data problem that would have caught earlier peer-city denominator bugs sooner: `population_year` in `data/peer-cities.csv` is misleadingly set to `2025` even where the actual population value comes from ACS 2023 or older Canadian data.
