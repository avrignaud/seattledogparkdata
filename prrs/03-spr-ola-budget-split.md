## Subject

Requesting OLA-specific expenditure data within BSL BC-PR-50000 "Maintaining Parks & Facilities," 2023–2026

## Body

Hello,

I am requesting public records documenting Seattle Parks & Recreation spending on **dog off-leash areas (OLAs) specifically**, separate from the P-Patch community-garden spending that is also funded through Budget Summary Level **BC-PR-50000 "Maintaining Parks & Facilities."**

During Seattle Park District Cycle 1 (2015–2020), SPR publicly stated that the OLA portion of this BSL was $100,000 per year. For Cycle 2 (2023–2028), the BSL has grown substantially (approximately $475K in 2023, $614K in 2024, $1.8M in 2025 proposed, $1.8M in 2026 proposed), and the published budget does not separate the OLA share from the P-Patch share.

I am requesting:
1. **Annual OLA-only operating and improvement spending within BC-PR-50000, from 2023 to present** (actuals for closed fiscal years, current-budget for the active year). If this is not tracked as a discrete line, please indicate that explicitly.
2. **Annual P-Patch-only spending within the same BSL over the same period**, so the remaining share is transparent.
3. **Any internal allocation methodology** used to split BC-PR-50000 spending between OLA and P-Patch work — e.g. staff hours, acreage-weighted allocation, or project tracking.
4. **Cycle 2 one-time capital spending on OLA construction** separate from BC-PR-50000, including the ~$3.1M allocation for West Seattle Stadium OLA and Othello Playground OLA.

This data is for a public-interest civic-data project at seattledogparkdata.com analyzing Seattle's OLA system. If these records live with the Park District rather than SPR directly, please route accordingly.

Thank you,

Andre Vrignaud
seattledogparkdata@ozymandi.as
