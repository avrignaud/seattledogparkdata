# PRR #5 — Per-OLA usage / headcount data

**Recipient:** Seattle Parks & Recreation · `PKS_PDR@seattle.gov`

**Status:** Draft — low priority (SPR likely does not track this systematically)

---

## Subject

Requesting any SPR-held usage counts, observation studies, or headcount data for individual off-leash areas

## Body

Hello,

I am requesting any records held by Seattle Parks & Recreation that document **observed usage** of individual off-leash areas (OLAs). Specifically:

1. **Point-in-time headcounts, attendance surveys, or observation studies** at any of Seattle's 14 existing OLAs, conducted by SPR staff or by contractors.
2. **Any data produced during or as part of the 2014–2017 People, Dogs and Parks Strategic Plan** or the 2023–2024 OLA Expansion Study that includes usage metrics (visits, headcounts, peak-hour density) by OLA.
3. **Internal maintenance reports or complaint logs** that reference specific OLAs by visit volume or capacity strain.
4. Any **volunteer or community-partner data** submitted to SPR (for example, from the Magnuson Off-Leash Group or Citizens for Off-Leash Areas) that includes usage counts.

If SPR does not systematically collect or retain this data, a simple response confirming that fact is sufficient — the absence is itself informative.

This data will be used for a public-interest civic-data project at seattledogparkdata.com analyzing Seattle's off-leash area system, specifically to evaluate whether the small OLAs (Kinnear at 0.1 acre, Plymouth Pillars at 0.15 acre, Regrade at 0.2 acre) are operating above their realistic capacity using the 75–100 square feet per dog industry planning standard.

Thank you,

Andre Vrignaud
seattledogparkdata@ozymandi.as

## Analysis purpose

- If SPR has any headcount data, even partial, it converts the "Kinnear is overcapacity" argument from theoretical (based on AKC and industry planning standards) to empirical (observed N dogs in 4,356 sq ft at time T).
- If SPR has no such data: that's a finding worth publishing — the agency manages 14 OLAs without measuring whether they are used to capacity.

## Fallback if no SPR data exists

Contact Citizens for Off-Leash Areas (COLA) and the Magnuson Off-Leash Group (MOLG) directly — both organizations have historically run informal counts.
