# PRR #2 — Find It Fix It "dog in a park" complaints by year

**Recipient:** Seattle Public Utilities (FIFI system owner) · `SPUPublicDisclosure@seattle.gov`

**Status:** Filed (awaiting response)

---

## Subject

Find It Fix It complaint data — dog-related park reports, 2015 to present

## Body

Hello,

I am requesting public records covering complaints filed through the City of Seattle's **Find It Fix It** app and web portal that relate to dogs in Seattle parks. Specifically:

1. **Total count of complaints categorized as "Dangerous Dog," "Dog in a Park," "Off-Leash Dog," or similar dog-related categories**, broken down by:
   - Year (calendar year, 2015 through present)
   - Complaint category (whatever internal category labels FIFI uses)
   - Park or location where reported, if captured
2. Any available summary reports, dashboards, or presentations that aggregate this data.
3. The list of FIFI complaint categories that relate to dogs, with brief definitions of each.

If the dataset contains personally identifying information about reporters or subjects, redacted aggregate counts are acceptable — I do not need names or contact details of individual reporters.

This data will be used for a public-interest civic-data project at seattledogparkdata.com analyzing Seattle's off-leash area system. If another department (Seattle Animal Control, SPR, SPD) is a better owner of this record set, please route accordingly.

Thank you,

Andre Vrignaud
seattledogparkdata@ozymandi.as

## Analysis purpose

Publicly cited figures put FIFI "dog in a park" complaints at approximately 1,100 in 2024 (source: secondary, not verified). An official annual time series would:
- Replace the approximate figure currently used on the site
- Show whether complaints are rising, flat, or falling as Seattle's dog population grows
- Potentially correlate with enforcement data from PRR C049204 + extension
