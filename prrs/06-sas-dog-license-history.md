## Request

Under Washington's Public Records Act (RCW 42.56), I am requesting the following records from the Seattle Animal Shelter (SAS):

1. **Annual count of active dog licenses issued by the Seattle Animal Shelter, for calendar years 2014 through the most recent complete year available.** Broken down by year; if possible, by ZIP code and by altered/unaltered status.

2. **Annual count of new dog licenses issued** (as distinct from total active licenses) for the same period, so I can distinguish renewal rates from new enrollments.

3. **SAS's current published estimate of compliance rate** — i.e., what percentage of Seattle dogs does SAS estimate are licensed vs. unlicensed. If SAS publishes or uses a compliance estimate methodology in internal planning, please include the source document or analysis.

4. **Annual dog-license fee revenue** collected by SAS, 2014 through the most recent complete fiscal year. Broken out from any other animal-license revenue (cats, goats, pigs).

5. **Any internal SAS analysis or presentation** that estimates Seattle's total dog population (both licensed and unlicensed), produced 2020 or later.

## Purpose (not a required disclosure, but provided for context)

I'm publishing a civic-data analysis of Seattle's off-leash dog area system at seattledogparkdata.com. I already have the current active-license count (26,652 dogs as of most recent data.seattle.gov snapshot, dataset `jguv-t9rb`), but the historical trend over 2014–2025, ZIP-level distribution, and any SAS-internal compliance estimate would materially improve the population floor I cite. License count is one of three estimates I plan to publish side-by-side (a licensed floor, an AVMA-derived demographic estimate, and SPR's 2023 Expansion Study upper bound); SAS's internal estimate, if one exists, would add a fourth independent data point.
