## Subject

## Body

Hi COLA team,

I'm Andre Vrignaud, a long-time Upper Queen Anne resident and Seattle dog owner. I participated in SPR's 2014–2017 *People, Dogs and Parks Strategic Plan* interview process as a community member.

I recently launched a civic-data project at **[seattledogparkdata.com](https://seattledogparkdata.com)** that pulls together everything I could find in the public record on Seattle's off-leash area system — supply, per-capita comparison with peer cities, acreage, walkability, budget allocation, the Kinnear Park case study, and SPR's own enforcement data (obtained through public records request C049204, 4,803 citations 2014–2019). The site is meant to be a neutral, primary-source-linked reference, plus one clearly marked opinion page for my own recommendations.

COLA comes up repeatedly in the sources I'm citing — your biennial reports, testimony during the 2014–2017 planning process, and your long-running advocacy on the issue. I'm writing to ask two things:

1. **Do you have usage-count or observation data** from any Seattle OLA — headcounts at peak hours, informal attendance surveys, anything systematic or semi-systematic? I'd like to evaluate how close Seattle's small OLAs (especially Kinnear, Plymouth Pillars, Regrade) are running to the industry-standard 75–100 square feet per dog capacity limit, and SPR appears not to measure this.

2. **Any corrections or additions** you'd suggest. The site cites COLA's 2017 biennial report and the acreage numbers derived from it. If you have an updated inventory, newer reports, or know of places where my data is off, I'd very much like to get it right.

Happy to share a preview of the site privately before it goes more public, and happy to credit COLA wherever you'd like. My goal here is to make the factual case for policy change — especially a shared-use / time-zoned policy of the sort discussed during the 2014 process — a defensible one, so that when advocates like COLA are at the table, the data is ready.

Thanks for the years of work on this.
