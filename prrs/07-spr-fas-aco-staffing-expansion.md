## Subject

Requesting the most recent MOU / MOA / inter-departmental agreements supporting the 2026 expansion of Animal Control Officer and park-ranger staffing for dog-related enforcement in Seattle parks

## Body

Hello,

I am requesting public records documenting the current (2025–2026) inter-departmental agreements and associated expenditure authorizations under which Seattle Parks & Recreation is expanding Animal Control Officer coverage for park-based dog enforcement.

On April 17, 2026, [Axios Seattle reported](https://www.axios.com/local/seattle/2026/04/17/more-paw-patrols-seattle-ramping-up-dog-related-enforcement) that SPR and the Seattle Animal Shelter are moving from a single Animal Control Officer covering Wednesday–Saturday to **two full-time seven-day positions plus backup support**, alongside the existing park-ranger corps. I have the [2016 MOA](../data/moas/SPR-FAS-ACO2-MOA-2016-04-02.pdf) and the [signed 2021 MOA AG21-PRF03-032](../data/moas/SPR-FAS-ACO2-MOA-2021-AG21-PRF03-032.pdf) on file — those documents establish the prior one-ACO-II arrangement and its $152,399/year FAS-side fully-loaded cost. I am requesting the documents that supersede or amend those agreements and that authorize the current expanded posture.

Specifically, I am requesting:

1. **The current MOU / MOA between SPR and the Seattle Animal Shelter** (and/or FAS, if that's the responsible department) governing Animal Control Officer assignment to park patrols, including any amendments, addenda, or successor agreements that replaced AG21-PRF03-032.

2. **Attachment A or equivalent** from that current agreement — the per-FTE billing rate, benefits-overhead multiplier, supplies and overhead allocation, and annual fully-loaded cost for each ACO position assigned to SPR.

3. **Total current authorized FTE count** for ACO positions assigned to park patrols, with effective date, and the total annual cost to SPR (both FAS-billed and any SPR-side paired positions such as the Facilities Maintenance Worker roles referenced in the 2021 MOA).

4. **The 2025 and 2026 budget line items** that fund this expansion — BSL / program / ledger codes — so the commitment is traceable in the adopted budget books.

5. **Any internal planning documents, memos, or council presentations** that accompanied the staffing decision, if public-record-eligible (not seeking deliberative-privileged material).

This data is for a public-interest civic-data project at [seattledogparkdata.com](https://seattledogparkdata.com/) analyzing Seattle's OLA system and the cost of its enforcement program. If any of the above records live with FAS, the Seattle Animal Shelter, or the Mayor's Office budget staff rather than SPR directly, please route accordingly and advise so I can file parallel requests.

Thank you,

Andre Vrignaud
seattledogparkdata@ozymandi.as
