## Subject

Seeking your input — seattledogparkdata.com and the Queen Anne OLA story

## Body

Hi Don,

I'm Andre Vrignaud — Queen Anne resident, dog owner, and longtime reader of your advocacy on OLA expansion in our neighborhood. I participated in the 2014&ndash;2017 SPR *People, Dogs and Parks Strategic Plan* interview process as a community member, and I've been following the thread of dog-park issues locally for about 20 years now myself.

I've just stood up **[seattledogparkdata.com](https://seattledogparkdata.com)**, a public-data reference on Seattle's OLA system. It pulls together everything I could find in the public record — SPR's inventory, the Seattle Park District financial plans, TPL 2025 ParkScore, Washington OFM population estimates, 4,803 off-leash citations obtained via public records request C049204, the signed 2021 SPR/FAS MOA funding the Animal Control Officer program, the 2024&ndash;25 OLA Expansion Study, and a network-distance walkshed I computed against OpenStreetMap and the 2020 Census. The site is deliberately neutral on the factual pages, with one clearly-marked opinion page that argues for a time-zoned shared-use policy as the realistic path forward, given land constraints.

You come up repeatedly in the source material I'm drawing from. I've preserved a summary of your comments in the <a href="https://github.com/avrignaud/seattledogparkdata/blob/main/sources/nextdoor-qacc-thread-2021.md">2021 Nextdoor Queen Anne Playfield thread source doc</a> — including the 20-year advocacy history, the Smith Cove OLA design reduction (55,000 sq ft → 25,000 sq ft), the MacLean Park attempt that was blocked by funding-source restrictions, the David Rogers Park pushback, and the original siting of Kinnear. Those are factual details I don't have elsewhere and that clearly matter to the story of how we got here.

I'm writing to ask three things:

1. **Corrections.** If I've got any of the QACC-related history wrong — sites, dates, amounts, who pushed back, what SPR agreed to — please tell me. The site will be updated with attribution.

2. **Documents or data you'd be willing to share.** Specifically: any QACC correspondence with SPR about OLA siting that you'd consider fair game for a public-interest civic-data project (redacted where appropriate); any community-assembled headcount or usage data for Kinnear or Magnolia Manor; and anything from the 2008 Pro Parks Levy period that's relevant to the Queen Anne sites.

3. **Connections.** Who at SPR (or, historically, at Andrew Lewis's office and its successors) has been the most substantive partner on OLA expansion? I'd like to approach them with specific questions about the current 2026 "Making Parks Safer" enforcement expansion and the OLA-only budget split that SPR has stopped disclosing.

My goal is to make the case for policy change — specifically a shared-use / time-zoned OLA model — as defensible as possible, so that when QACC, COLA, or individual advocates are at the table, the data is ready. I'm not interested in publishing private correspondence and I'm happy to treat anything you share with whatever confidentiality you'd like.

Thanks for the two decades of work on this. Happy to meet in person (coffee on 1st W) or by email — whatever's easiest.
