May 2026 · Enforcement

2016-program-expansion finding added

Citation output rose roughly seven-fold between 2014 and 2018, after the SPR/FAS Animal Control Officer + Facilities Maintenance Worker patrol team went full-time under the April 2016 MOA. The available data does not show a corresponding change in off-leash behavior across the same period — the repeat-offender share stayed flat at 13%, self-reported illegal use stayed at 39% (SPR's own 2016 survey), and Find It Fix It complaints continued to rise into 2024.

The new section synthesizes existing PRR C049204 data; no new dataset was ingested. The data-currency banner on the enforcement page was updated to note that the dataset's October 2019 cutoff falls just before the COVID period and the 2021 MOA renewal — the same window during which SPR planned its 2026 patrol expansion.

A new public records request (PRR #8) was filed asking SPR/FAS for any internal evaluation of the 2016 program expansion, patrol-deployment logs, the decision-record for the 2026 expansion, and community-partner correspondence on program effectiveness. Whichever way the response comes back — documented evaluation, or absence of one — lets the section be replaced with a documented finding.

April 2026 · Site launch (v1.0.0)

First complete public release

Eight-page public-data reference on Seattle's off-leash area system: overview, Part I (The Gap), Part II (Access), Part III (Forward), Enforcement, Budget, Peer Cities, and the signed Opinion editorial. Every factual claim links back to its source; the underlying dataset pipeline is reproducible from committed scripts.

Network walkshed methodology landed as part of v1.0. The earlier ~33% straight-line walkshed estimate was replaced with a proper network-distance analysis: 11.7% of Seattle residents are within a 10-minute walking path of an OLA (76.6% within SPR's 2.5-mile standard). Computed via compute_walkshed.py (osmnx against Seattle's OSM walk network) and population_coverage.py (2020 Census block-group overlay).

Enforcement page shipped with the full PRR C049204 dataset (4,803 citations, 2014–2019), hotspot map, walkshed-overlap analysis, top-20 table, offense-mix breakdown, and revenue-vs-cost chart against the 2021 MOA's documented $152,399/yr ACO II cost.

Peer Cities page shipped with detail sections on Portland, San Francisco, Vancouver BC, Washington DC, Minneapolis, NYC, Austin, and Boise.
