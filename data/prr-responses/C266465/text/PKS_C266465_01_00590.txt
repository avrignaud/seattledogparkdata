1.   INTRODUCTION
This Memorandum of Agreement ("Agreement") is made by and between Seattle Parks and Recreation
("SPR") and the Department of Finance and Administrative Services ("FAS"), representing its Seattle
Animal Shelter ("Shelter or SAS") within the Administrative Business Unit(ABU). This agreement
addresses scope of work, invoicing and billing, and reporting for the Making Parks Safer initiative, one of
the component elements of the broader Maintaining Parks and Facilities initiative funded by the Seattle
Park District ("Park District").


This Agreement is for the funding by SPR, through the Park District, of a 1.0 FTE Animal Control Officer II
("ACO") who is employed by the Shelter. The ACO will work in a team with a Facilities Maintenance
Worker ("FMW") employed by SPR. (The term "the ACO" encompasses the possibility that more than
one ACO may perform the work program defined here.) The team's work program is to educate the
public and enforce dog leash and scoop laws at SPR park locations, especially those locations with a
history of complaints, violations and impacts to users and/or natural resources based on park users'
non-compliance with these laws.


2.   TERM OF AGREEMENT
The term of this Agreement shall begin when fully executed by all parties and shall end on December 31,
2022, unless amended by written agreement or terminated earlier. This term is shorter than the prior
five-year MOA given the unprecedented circumstances and impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic although
it is anticipated that the work program described in this Agreement will continue for the remainder of
the five years of this Park District funding cycle and that the parties will mutually amend or renegotiate
this Agreement on an as-needed basis.


3.   BILLING AND PAYMENT


During the term of this Agreement, SPR will reimburse FAS for 80 hours/pay period at the top rate of
pay including Annual Wage Increases (AWI), and an additional 45% for all employee benefits. Overtime
is not included unless pre-authorized by SPR. The ACO II will remain on the FAS payroll and code hours
worked to a project code. Timesheets for the FMW will be approved by the SPR supervisor and
timesheets for the ACO will be approved by the SAS Field Services Supervisor.

FAS will submit quarterly requests for payment to SPR via a pre-established interdepartmental journal

4.   WORK SCHEDULE
The typical work schedule described in this Agreement will take place on a Wednesday through Saturday
or Sunday through Wednesday schedule. Work hours will vary by season and programmatically, based
on high-use times and in order to maximize the number of daylight hours the team can spend in parks.
The typical hours will be 0900 to 1900 during daylight savings time and 0600 to 1600 when daylight
savings ends. The team may need to flex their hours to attend community meetings to discuss parks
related topics. The ACO and FMW will either report to or meet at the Shelter and then depart for the
day's planned itinerary or alternatively, by mutual agreement, at a SPR's maintenance facility in a
location determined to suit the needs of the program. The FMW and ACO will share driving
responsibilities of both FAS and SPR vehicles and each program team member shall have a key for the
vehicle being used that day. Repairs of damage to department vehicles will be the responsibility of the
department of the driver when the damage occurred. The ACO and the FMW will spend the majority of
their shift engaged in the work described in this agreement, including the time spent to travel to and
between parks and to return to either the Shelter or other designated location at the end of their shift.
An exception will be for department or program required trainings, meetings and other designated
events.


Note - Due to the variable, fluid, and unprecedented impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic staffing needs
with both SPR and SAS may require modification of this work schedule during the term of this
Agreement. If SPR and/or SAS are unable to meet the requirements listed above the SAS manager of
field services and the SPR security supervisor shall provide written notification of the reason and
expected resumption of work schedule.


5.   SCOPE OF WORK
While they work as a team, the ACO and the FMW will seek to educate park users and promote
compliance with all applicable animal-control laws in SPR parks. Enforcement of these laws is within the
purview of the Shelter, and the ACO will have the discretionary authority to issue citations for
non-compliance. It is anticipated, however, that the work program will be primarily educational and that
park users' observation of the ACO/FMW team will have a deterrent effect in terms of non-compliance.
The FMW may also carry out identified or ad hoc security service in the parks; enforce the City Park
Code; provide basic customer service to park users; collect information about park use, conditions, and
more complex service and/or maintenance needs; and other duties as assigned. The ACO may also carry
out FAS/SAS identified, or ad hoc service tasks as assigned by the Shelter leadership team; and other
duties as assigned. SPR and FAS will establish guidelines by which they will create the team's work

6.   CONDITIONS OF WORK
While in a park, the FMW and ACO will always maintain radio communication with each other with an
emphasis to keep a line-of-sight contact at all times and be ready to move into close physical proximity
should an interaction with a park user become charged. The FMW and the ACO should view themselves
as a team, working together and communicating with each other to address issues of concern to both
SPR and FAS. The ACO shall be responsible for organizing the daily patrol area and route based on
complaints received by SAS. Other duties performed by this team shall be shared. SAS will process and
hold all law enforcement agency copies of citations for the required retention period. SPR will be
responsible for supplying the FMW with a citation book from SPD. The ACO will carry barrier
equipment, which may be used as a tool or barrier to push away an aggressive animal. The FMW will
carry similar protective equipment. The ACO and FMW will both carry cell phones as well as any other
equipment mutually agreed to by SPR and FAS.


7.   DOCUMENTATION AND REPORTING
SPR will provide the FMW with the electronic equipment needed to record the origin and type of
interaction with a member of the public, the infraction observed, and the result of the interaction (e.g.,
patrol and incident; scoop violation; warning). SPR will also collect data on the location and time of each
interaction and on each visit by the team to a single park. SPR may also collect other points of data that
reasonably relate to the work program and its goal of Making Parks Safer. If the electronic means to
collect data are not available due to equipment unavailability or failure, SPR will provide the FMW with
the means to collect this data manually. In that case SPR will be responsible for transferring the data to
the appropriate system and for returning the electronic equipment to use as soon as possible. The ACO
will provide SAS management weekly data on the number of parks patrolled, tickets issued, warnings
issued, pet licenses sold, and licensing warnings issued, including dollar amounts on the total number of
citations and licenses sold.


Within five working days of the end of each month, SPR will provide to FAS a summary report on that
month's data, which FAS will include with its invoice to SPR Accounting.


SAS will supply SPR Security with quarterly statistics including, numbers of contacts, verbal and written
warnings, citation written and other pertinent information to be used as performance measures,
reporting and record keeping.


8.   ASSIGNMENT
This Agreement shall not be assigned in whole or in part by either party without the prior written
approval of the other party.


9.   COMPLIANCE WITH LAW
The parties to this agreement shall comply with all federal, state, and local laws and ordinances.
