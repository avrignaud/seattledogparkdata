Hi Justin,

Given our conversation yesterday about how our hands are tied on the dog walker at

concerning that people who are off-leash at non off-leash parks are being threatened with
exclusion orders. We have received multiple reports of exclusion orders, trespasses, and

Frankly, enforcing policies in this manner seems discriminatory and makes it appear like SPR
and SPR enforcement are more concerned about dogs being off-leash where they shouldn't be
than they are about actual aggressive and dangerous dogs being brought into legitimate off-
leash areas, putting other dogs and people at risk. If it is so difficult to exclude someone that
is knowingly breaking the rules inside an off-leash area and putting the lives of people and
dogs at risk, why is it so easy to exclude someone who is off-leash where they shouldn't be?
