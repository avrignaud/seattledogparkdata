Hello, fleet colleagues -
(bcc: Citywide fleet coordinators, managers, and advisors)

I'm reaching out to let you know of changes to our billing processes. Starting this year, the charges for
Equipment Planning and Procurement (EPPS) and Lease Maintenance will no longer be processed as monthly
recurring charges in Fleet Anywhere. Instead, FAS Accounting and Budgeting Services (ABS) will handle these
charges as direct allocations in PeopleSoft.

This change is being made to align actuals with what is Council-adopted and published in the Central Cost
Manual (CCM). The previous practice of charging rates per vehicle led to over or under collection due to the
dynamic nature of vehicles being added or disposed of from the fleet. With this new approach, the standard
allocations by department will remain consistent each month, with charges distributed to the asset/vehicle
level in PeopleSoft. While the monthly totals will be the same, the distribution and resulting charge per asset
may change if your fleet count is increased or reduced.

As a result, you will no longer see a monthly EPPS or Maintenance fee in Fleet Anywhere (see the example
below). However, you can still access these charges and asset details in PeopleSoft. Additionally, Lucy is
developing a PowerBI report that will allow us to pull this data outside of PeopleSoft. Please note that Monthly
Capital Lease rates will continue to be processed in Fleet Anywhere with no changes.

These updates should have already been communicated by FAS ABS to your departmental finance teams. If
you have any concerns about impacts on specialized reports your teams have built in PeopleSoft or other
systems, I encourage you to reach out to your departmental representatives. I've attached the adopted CCM
by function for your reference, and you can filter for your department using the Updated Payor filter. The
relevant CCM functions are the Fleet Engineering & Planning and Preventative Maintenance (Lease
Maintenance) line items.

Please feel free to reach out with any questions or concerns, thanks!
