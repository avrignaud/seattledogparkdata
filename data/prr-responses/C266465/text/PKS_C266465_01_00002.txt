Re:               2014 Statement of Legislative Intent - Dog Off Leash Area Master Plan (69-1-
                  B-l)


The 2014 Adopted Budget included a City Council Statement of Legislative Intent (SLI)
regarding off leash areas (OLAs). Specifically, the SLI requested that the Department work
in conjunction with the Citizens for Off Leash Areas (COLA) and other stakeholders to
create a dog off leash area (OLA) master plan and present the plan to Council by October 1,
2014. The purpose of the OLA master plan is to identify a long term plan for the City's
existing OLA's, as well as for maintenance, acquisition and expansion of OLA projects.

In May, 2014, Seattle Parks and Recreation (SPR) submitted a request to wait until the Park
District was approved so that funding included in the District (about $100,000 annually)
could be used to fund the master plan efforts. Council agreed with that approach and asked
the department to provide an interim report in May of 2015 and a final report by
November 1, 2015. The interim report was provided on May 15, 2015. In November, the
Citizens for Off Leash Areas (COLA) asked SPR to request a further delay until June, 2016 to
allow more time for research and the Council concurred in that request.

SPR is now submitting the Draft People, Dogs and Parks Plan in fulfillment of the original
SLI.

The Draft People, Dog and Parks Plan (Plan) is a long term plan which will guide the
operations of existing OLAs, explore alternative service models and create strategies for
development of future OLAs. It provides direction for how to spend Park District funding
designated for OLAs over the approved six year Park District spending plan (2016-2020).

As the Plan was developed, SPR consulted with COLA, dog walkers and other interested
parties through direct consultation, surveys of dog owners, focus groups of OLA supporters
and Seattle residents, and a Board of Park Commissioner's public hearing. The 2015
Recreation Demand Study included specific questions related to dog owners and reported
on both current and projected participation rates. COLA prepared a Best Practices Report
which is referenced in the Plan and appended, and the COLA Biennial Report (appended)
recounts the twenty year history of COLA's involvement with OLA development along with
recommendations for improving existing OLAs and fundraising to help with their support
and development.

SPR submits the Dogs, People and Parks Plan in draft form to allow for more public
review and comment which will occur this summer.
