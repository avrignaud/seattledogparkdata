Background:

(SPR) and the Department of Finance and Administrative Services (FAS), representing its Seattle Animal
Shelter. The agreement supports the work of the 'Making Parks Safer' initiative. The current agreement
expires on December 31, 2027. The agreement is funded by SPR and the Park District, for three (3) Animal
Control Officers II positions employed by Seattle Animal Shelter. The Animal Control Officers work in a
team with a SPR Facilities Maintenance Worker. [Note, the current FMW position have accepted a Park
Ranger position in 2023]. There is no current FMW working with the Animal Control Officers. The team's
work program is to educate the public and enforce - leash and scoop laws at SPR park locations, especially
those locations with a history of complaints, violations and impacts to users and/or natural resources
based on park users' non-compliance with these laws. [Note: since 2023 Park Rangers have been
educating the public on the Park Codes and Rules, including the leash, animals running at large, and the

 1.       PURPOSE
 This Memorandum of Agreement ("Agreement") is made by and between Seattle Parks and Recreation
 ("SPR") and the Department of Finance and Administrative Services ("FAS"), representing its Seattle
 Animal Shelter ("SAS"). This agreement supports the work of the Making Parks Safer initiative, one of the
 component elements of the broader Maintaining Parks and Facilities initiative funded by the Seattle Park
 District ("Park District"). The agreement addresses scope of work, schedule and conditions of work,
 reporting and compensation and billing.


 This Agreement is for the funding by SPR and the Park District, of three ACO II ("ACO") positions
 employed by SAS. (The term "the ACO" encompasses the fact that more than one ACO may perform the
 work program defined here.) The ACO will work in a team with a Seattle Park Ranger Facilities
 Maintenance Worker ("FMW") employed by SPR. The team's work program is to educate the public and
 enforce - leash and scoop laws at SPR park locations, especially those locations with a history of
 complaints, violations and impacts to users and/or natural resources based on park users' non-
 compliance with these laws. [Note: Park Rangers have been educating the public at the parks during
 their scheduled routes].


 2.       SCOPE OF WORK
 While working as a team, the ACO and a Park Ranger FMW will seek to educate park users and promote
 compliance with all applicable animal-control laws in SPR parks. Enforcement of these laws is within the
 purview of SAS, and the ACO will have the discretionary authority to issue citations for non-compliance.
 It is anticipated, however, that the work program will be primarily educational and that park users'
 observation of the ACO/Park Ranger FMW team will have a deterrent effect in terms of non-compliance.
 The Park Ranger FMW may also carry out identified or ad hoc service and/or maintenance tasks will also
 identify any park maintenance service needs and call the SPR Dispatcher to create a WO for the

 maintenance task in the parks; enforce the City Park Code; provide basic customer service to park users;
 collect information about park use, conditions, and more complex service and/or maintenance needs;

 The ACO may also carry out FAS/SAS identified, or ad hoc service tasks as assigned by the SAS leadership
 team; and other duties as assigned. SPR and FAS will establish guidelines by which they will create the
 team's work itineraries and the degree to which these itineraries will remain flexible, for instance to
 respond to emergent issues and/or complaints.


 3.   WORK SCHEDULE
 The typical work schedule described in this Agreement will take place on a Wednesday through Saturday
 or Sunday through Wednesday schedule. Work hours will vary by season and programmatically, based on
 high-use times and to maximize the number of daylight hours the team can spend in parks. The typical
 hours will be 1000 to 2000 during daylight savings time and 0700 to 1700 when daylight savings ends.
 The team may also need to flex their hours to attend community meetings to discuss park-related topics.

The ACO and Park Ranger FMW will either report to or meet at the SAS and then depart for the day's
planned itinerary or alternatively, by mutual agreement, at a SPR's maintenance facility in a location
determined to suit the needs of the program. The Park Ranger FMW and ACO will share driving
responsibilities of both FAS and SPR vehicles and each program team member shall have a key for the
vehicle being used that day.
 Repairs of damage to department vehicles will be the responsibility of the department of the driver when
 the damage occurred. The ACO and the Park Ranger FMW will spend most of their shift engaged in the
 work described in this Agreement, including the time spent to travel to and between parks and to return
 to either SAS or other designated location at the end of their shift. An exception will be for department or
 program required trainings, meetings and other designated events.


 4.   CONDITIONS OF WORK
 While in a park, the Park Ranger EMW and ACO will always maintain radio communication with each
 other with an emphasis to always keep a line-of-sight contact and be ready to move into close physical
 proximity should an interaction with a park user become charged. The Park Ranger FMW and the ACO
 should view themselves as a team, working together and communicating with each other to address
 issues of concern to both SPR and FAS. The ACO shall be responsible for organizing the daily patrol area
 and route based on complaints received by SAS. Other duties performed by this team shall be shared.
 SAS will process and hold all law enforcement agency copies of citations issued by ACOsfor the required
 retention period. SPR will process and hold all law enforcement agency copies of citations issued by the
 Park Ranger FMWs for the required retention period. SPR will be responsible for supplying the Park
 Ranger FMW with a citation book from SPD. SPR will provide a copy of citations issued for leash law
 violations by the Park Ranger FMW-to SAS for recording in the SAS database. The ACO will carry barrier
 equipment, which may be used as a tool or barrier to push away an aggressive animal. The Park Ranger
 FMW will carry similar protective equipment. The ACO and Park Ranger FMW will both carry cell phones
 as well as any other equipment mutually agreed to by SPR and FAS.
