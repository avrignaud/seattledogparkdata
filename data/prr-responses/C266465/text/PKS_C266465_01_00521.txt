                             On 5/20/2022 between 1615hrs and 1705hrs I patrolled the most populous 1/2 mile of Alki Beach Park on foot. I made three soft
                             contacts with individuals with dogs on-leash on the upper portion of the beach. All three left the beach immediately.



     Comments:               I also made three contacts with individuals with off-leash dogs. The first leashed his dog and left the beach without speaking to me.
                             refusing to identify himself. He stayed on the concrete sidewalk above the beach until I left, at which time I observed him walk back
                             down to the beach with his dog. The second individual also refused to identify herself, stating, "Everybody down here knows you
                             guys are a joke and can't do anything. Go ahead and make me identify myself." She leashed her dog but did not leave the beach
                             with the dog. The third individual stated that she knew she didn't have to say anything to me, refused to identify herself, walked with
                             the dog to the bathrooms, then returned to the beach and let the dog off leash once I as out of the immediate area.
