5. DOCUMENTATION AND REPORTING SPR will provide the Park Ranger with the
electronic equipment needed to record the origin and type of interaction with a
member of the public, the infraction observed, and the result of the interaction, (i.e.,
patrol and incident; scoop violation; warning). SPR will also collect data on the
location and time of each interaction and on each visit by the team to a single park.
SPR may also collect other points of data that reasonably relate to the work program
and its goals. The Park Ranger will enter the data into the Park Ranger Data
Tracking App. SPR will share the data collected with FAS. The ACO and Park
Ranger shall record all contacts and citations in the SPR app on the Parks Code
Violation Dashboard. FAS will provide access to PetPoint and training for Park
Rangers from ACOs on ticket writing and utilizing PetPoint to track contacts. A
regular reporting mechanism will be vital for tracking progress and outcomes.
Monthly reports from ACOs should include park complaints received, the number of
warnings and citations issued, and the number of patrols completed by ACOs, both
with and without Park Rangers. Within five working days of the end of each month,
