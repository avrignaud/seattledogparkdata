              2022 Seattle City Council Statement of Legislative Intent Council Budget Action
                                   SPR PED/Security Response Outline


Budget Action Title: Request that FAS report on increased efficiencies in animal control patrolling
Legislative Intent: This Statement of Legislative Intent (SLI) would request that the Department of
Finance and Administrative Services (FAS) provide a report on potential increases in efficiencies that
would increase the time spent by, and total number of Animal Control Officer II positions dedicated to,
the enforcement of relevant City ordinances in City parks. The report should include sufficient
information to allow Council to evaluate potential means to increase parks enforcement by Animal
Control Officers while minimizing negative impacts on other duties, including those benefiting homeless
and underserved communities and responding to animal cruelty and aggressive animal issues. The
report should also include an evaluation of the potential outcomes of increased use of informational
materials and expansion of community-based services. Overall, the report would be useful to the extent
that it helps inform future Council decisions supporting better enforcement of leash and scoop laws in
City parks. The report should be submitted to the Chair of the Public Safety and Human Services
Committee, by June 30, 2022.

SPR Response Outline:

    1.   To support increase efficiencies with FAS, the following actions will continue and/or will be
         implemented:

         a) Continue Interdepartmental MOU between SPR and FAS, resulting in collaboration,
            coordination and leveraging of resources. SPR annual contribution $ funding of XXX ACO II
            to patrol OLAs. FAS annual contribution $XXX ACO II.
         b) SPR Collector APP that provide mobile use and real time posting of OLA violations/citations
            and reporting. FAS will work with SPR to make sure ACO Ils are using the app, which
            includes training from Seattle IT.
         c) SPR and FAS will also develop a reporting platform that both departments will use to track
            and post the number of hours devoted to these efforts and where they are spent per Parks
            Assets.
         d) Monthly meetings between SPR/FAS to coordinate and assess progress and develop hard
            emphasis assets to patrol.
         e) SPR Security will work with COLA to develop and recruit volunteers that can be utilized to
            educate and build stewardship around code of conduct and OLA behavior etiquette.
         f) SPR Park District Security Proposal:
            i)      This proposal, if approved, will add $200,000 to fund 2 additional FAS ACOs to
                     support OLA enforcement. This will result in a total of 4 ACOs (3 from FAS and 1
                     from SPR) or double the number of ACOs to these efforts.
              ii)    This would also mean that an additional 3200 direct labor hours will be contributing
                     to these efforts for a total of 6,400 hours per year.
              iii)   This will also result in an estimated XXXXX number of verbal citations, and XXXXX
                     written citations for OLA enforcement
