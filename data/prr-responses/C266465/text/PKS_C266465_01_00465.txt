1.   PURPOSE
This Memorandum of Agreement ("Agreement") is made by and between Seattle Parks and Recreation
("SPR") and the Department of Finance and Administrative Services ("FAS"), representing its Seattle
Animal Shelter ("SAS"). This agreement supports the work of the Making Parks Safer initiative, one of the
component elements of the broader Maintaining Parks and Facilities initiative funded by the Seattle Park
District ("Park District"). The agreement addresses scope of work, schedule and conditions of work,
reporting and compensation and billing.


This Agreement is for the funding by SPR and the Park District, of three ACO II ("ACO") positions
employed by SAS. (The term "the ACO" encompasses the fact that more than one ACO may perform the
work program defined here.) The ACO will work in a team with a Facilities Maintenance Worker ("FMW")
employed by SPR. The team's work program is to educate the public and enforce - leash and scoop laws
at SPR park locations, especially those locations with a history of complaints, violations and impacts to
users and/or natural resources based on park users' non-compliance with these laws.


2.   SCOPE OF WORK
While working as a team, the ACO and the FMW will seek to educate park users and promote compliance
with all applicable animal-control laws in SPR parks. Enforcement of these laws is within the purview of
SAS, and the ACO will have the discretionary authority to issue citations for non-compliance. It is
anticipated, however, that the work program will be primarily educational and that park users'
observation of the ACO/FMW team will have a deterrent effect in terms of non-compliance. The FMW
may also carry out identified or ad hoc service and/or maintenance tasks in the parks; enforce the City
Park Code; provide basic customer service to park users; collect information about park use, conditions,
and more complex service and/or maintenance needs; and other duties as assigned. The ACO may also
carry out FAS/SAS identified, or ad hoc service tasks as assigned by the SAS leadership team; and other
duties as assigned. SPR and FAS will establish guidelines by which they will create the team's work
itineraries and the degree to which these itineraries will remain flexible, for instance to respond to
emergent issues and/or complaints.


3.   WORK SCHEDULE
The typical work schedule described in this Agreement will take place on a Wednesday through Saturday
or Sunday through Wednesday schedule. Work hours will vary by season and programmatically, based on
high-use times and to maximize the number of daylight hours the team can spend in parks. The typical
hours will be 1000 to 2000 during daylight savings time and 0700 to 1700 when daylight savings ends.
The team may also need to flex their hours to attend community meetings to discuss park-related topics.

The ACO and FMW will either report to or meet at the SAS and then depart for the day's planned
itinerary or alternatively, by mutual agreement, at a SPR's maintenance facility in a location determined
to suit the needs of the program. The FMW and ACO will share driving responsibilities of both FAS and
SPR vehicles and each program team member shall have a key for the vehicle being used that day.
Repairs of damage to department vehicles will be the responsibility of the department of the driver when
the damage occurred. The ACO and the FMW will spend most of their shift engaged in the work
described in this Agreement, including the time spent to travel to and between parks and to return to
either SAS or other designated location at the end of their shift. An exception will be for department or
program required trainings, meetings and other designated events.


4.   CONDITIONS OF WORK
While in a park, the FMW and ACO will always maintain radio communication with each other with an
emphasis to keep a line-of-sight contact at all times and be ready to move into close physical proximity
should an interaction with a park user become charged. The FMW and the ACO should view themselves
as a team, working together and communicating with each other to address issues of concern to both
SPR and FAS. The ACO shall be responsible for organizing the daily patrol area and route based on
complaints received by SAS. Other duties performed by this team shall be shared. SAS will process and
hold all law enforcement agency copies of citations issued by ACOs for the required retention period. SPR
will process and hold all law enforcement agency copies of citations issued by FMWs for the required
retention period. SPR will be responsible for supplying the FMW with a citation book from SPD. SPR will
provide a copy of citations issued for leash law violations by FMW to SAS for recording in the SAS
database. The ACO will carry barrier equipment, which may be used as a tool or barrier to push away an
aggressive animal. The FMW will carry similar protective equipment. The ACO and FMW will both carry
cell phones as well as any other equipment mutually agreed to by SPR and FAS.


5.   DOCUMENTATION AND REPORTING
SPR will provide the FMW with the electronic equipment needed to record the origin and type of
interaction with a member of the public, the infraction observed, and the result of the interaction. (E.g.,
patrol and incident; scoop violation; warning). SPR will also collect data on the location and time of each
interaction and on each visit by the team to a single park. SPR may also collect other points of data that
reasonably relate to the work program and its goal of Making Parks Safer. If the electronic means to
collect data are not available due to equipment unavailability or failure, SPR will provide the FMW with
the means to collect this data manually. In that case SPR will be responsible for transferring the data to
the appropriate system and for returning the electronic equipment to use as soon as possible. The ACO
and FMW shall record all contacts and citations in the SPR app on the Parks Code Violation Dashboard.


Within five working days of the end of each month, SPR will provide FAS a summary report on that
month's data, which FAS will include with its invoice to SPR Accounting.


6.   TERM OF AGREEMENT
The term of this Agreement shall begin when fully executed by all parties and shall end on December 31,
2027, unless amended by written agreement or terminated earlier.

7.    BILLING AND PAYMENT
During the term of this Agreement, SPR will reimburse FAS for 240 hours/pay period (3 FTEs ACO Ils * 80
hours/pay period) at the applicable ACO II top hourly rate including Annual Wage Increases (AWI), and an
additional 47.97% for all ACO employee benefits. Overtime is not included unless pre-authorized by SPR.
The ACO will remain on the FAS payroll and code hours worked to a project code. Timesheets for the
FMW will be approved by the SPR supervisor and timesheets for the ACO will be approved by the SAS
Field Services Supervisor.


In addition, SPR will reimburse FAS program and supply costs of $9,000 ($3,000 each * 3 FTEs ACO II) and
divisional overhead of $30,495 ($10,165 each * 3 FTEs ACO II).
