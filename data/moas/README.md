# SPR × FAS Animal Control Officer MOAs — archive

Memoranda of Agreement between Seattle Parks & Recreation (SPR) and
Finance & Administrative Services (FAS, which houses the Seattle Animal
Shelter) that fund the Animal Control Officer II position(s) assigned to
park patrols under the "Making Parks Safer" initiative (one ACO II 2016–2022;
three ACO IIs *funded* from 2023 — FAS stated in Feb 2025 it could staff 1.5–2.0, and from
2025 the MOAs fund "up to three" rather than three).

> **Where the "up to three … any number of positions" language came from.** PRR
> [C266465](../prr-responses/C266465/) supplies the drafting history. In Sep 2024 SPR's own
> Community Services manager recommended **cutting** the program to 2 or 1 ACO (saving
> $151,550 / $303,101); a 2-ACO draft was circulated in Jan 2025; on 2025-02-24 the Interim
> Deputy Superintendent directed it back to 3 "given AP's priority with off leash"; two days
> later FAS said it could realistically staff **1.5–2.0 FTE**. The 2025 MOA's flexible
> headcount plus actual-hours billing is the compromise between a 3-FTE authorization and a
> 2-FTE workforce — and the 2026 MOA simply re-prices it. Read the two together.

> **Note on the 2026 MOA:** a distinct 2026 agreement *does* exist (received June 2026 via PRR C265589; filed above as `SPR-FAS-ACO2-MOA-2026.pdf`). It does not reference or supersede the 2023 MOA and carries its own "entire agreement" clause, so whether it formally replaces the 2023 instrument is unstated in the document. Substantively it re-prices the same three-ACO-II program for wage growth (per-FTE $151,551 → $176,093; 3-FTE $454,652 → $528,279) and changes the SPR-side paired role from FMW to **Park Ranger**. The expansion was **authorized in 2023**, not 2026; the April 2026 Axios "more paw patrols" reporting reflects the program filling its 2023-authorized positions (still largely vacant as of April 2026), not a new scope expansion.

## How to add a new MOA

1. Drop the PDF into this directory with the filename pattern `SPR-FAS-ACO2-MOA-<YYYY>-<ref>.pdf`.
2. Add a row to the table above describing what's in it and the headline cost or staffing numbers.
3. Update [`sources/aco-moa-2016.md`](../../sources/aco-moa-2016.md) — the consolidated methodology document.
4. Update any narrative references on the [Enforcement page](../../docs/enforcement.html#finding-06) and the [Opinion page](../../docs/opinion.html#O1) if the new document changes the per-year cost numbers.

## Why these MOAs matter for the site

These documents are the cost side of the enforcement program whose output
is captured in [`data/prr-responses/C049204/`](../prr-responses/C049204/)
(4,803 off-leash citations 2014–2019). Together they let the site say, with
primary-source backing, that the program is a net cost center (about 12–23%
cost recovery via fee revenue) rather than a revenue-generating enforcement
activity like parking. See the <a href="../../docs/enforcement.html#finding-06">Enforcement Finding 06</a>
for the full revenue-vs-cost analysis.
